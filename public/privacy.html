Enquiry privacy notice

Qwanta Global Sourcing uses the information you supply in the quotation form to evaluate your request, contact you and coordinate potential business. The form collects your name, company, business email, product requirement, quantity and destination, plus optional details. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Enquiries are stored on the website server and may be sent to the sourcing team by email. Technical logs may be retained for security and abuse prevention. Product images may be loaded from Wikimedia if local copies are unavailable.
